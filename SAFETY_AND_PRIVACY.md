# Safety, Privacy, and Outreach Rules

- Keep CVs, transcripts with personal identifiers, applicant contact details, tokens, OAuth files, and credentials out of public GitHub.
- A private repository is not a substitute for secret management.
- Use environment variables or a local ignored `.env` file; never commit `.env`.
- Use relevant publicly listed faculty contact information and respect university terms and applicable law.
- Do not bypass CAPTCHAs, authentication, rate limits, or technical access controls.
- Never invent faculty details, emails, papers, funding, deadlines, or recruiting status.
- Default to Gmail drafts; require human approval before sending a message or reviewed batch.
- Maintain a suppression list for opt-outs, bounces, and do-not-contact requests.
- Stop contacting people who opt out or ask not to be contacted.
- Avoid deceptive subject lines, false familiarity, and generic bulk emails.
- Make data retention and deletion straightforward.
