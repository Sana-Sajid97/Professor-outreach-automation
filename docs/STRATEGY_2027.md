# Strategy for a fully funded start in 2027 (assistantships only)

*Research date: 11 Oct 2026. Every fact below comes from official university pages found through web search and is listed with its source in `data/seed/programs.csv` and `data/seed/professors_leads.csv`. Many pages show recurring dates without a year ("Dec 1") or last year's cycle. **Confirm each deadline and requirement on the official page before applying.***

## The short version
1. **Fall 2027 is the main target.** The best fully funded PhD programs that accept IELTS 6.5 (or lower) mostly have **1–15 December 2026** deadlines, so the next seven weeks matter most.
2. **Spring 2027 is a long shot.** It is worth one week of focused emails to professors who could fund a research assistant from January (§3).
3. **Funding = a professor or department paying you a stipend and waiving tuition.** That is why you email professors before applying. At most mentor-based programs (Penn State, Texas Tech, LSU, Virginia Tech, Clemson, NC State, UConn), admission or funding depends on a faculty member agreeing to take you.
4. **IELTS 6.5 works for admission at the programs below, but usually not for teaching assistantships.** Research assistantships are your main route unless a retake raises your speaking score.

## 6. Costs to plan for
- Application fees: usually $60–$100 each. **Ask about fee waivers.** Many programs grant them to strong international applicants, and some professors can request one.
- English test, WES evaluation, SEVIS fee and visa fee. Budget for these before you get an offer.
